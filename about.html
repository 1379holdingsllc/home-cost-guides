About Home Cost Guides

Home Cost Guides is published by 1379 Holdings LLC, an Atlanta, Georgia business that works in cleaning, pressure washing, junk removal and related home and commercial services.

We started this site because homeowners and small businesses regularly ask us the same question: is this quote fair? National cost surveys are scattered across many websites and often disagree. Our guides pull the published ranges together in one place, cite every source, and add practical, field-level context about what really changes a price.

Our editorial approach

  • We do not invent statistics. Price figures are attributed to the published source they came from.
  • Ranges are presented as typical estimates, not quotes.
  • We update guides when sources publish new data.
  • Where we offer a service ourselves, we say so plainly; we still recommend getting more than one quote.
